Refund policy

Cancel anytime. Here is when and how you can get your money back.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

  1. 1. Cancel anytime
  2. 2. Your first payment
  3. 3. Renewals
  4. 4. Changing plans
  5. 5. When something went wrong on our side
  6. 6. Consumers in the EU and the UK
  7. 7. How refunds are paid
  8. 8. Contact

1. Cancel anytime

Go to Settings, then Plan and billing, then Manage billing, and cancel. There is no further charge. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you already paid for, then you are on Free. Your projects and scans stay.

2. Your first payment

If SafeFix is not right for you, write to support@safefix.dev within 14 days of your first payment for a plan. We refund that payment in full and move you to Free.

3. Renewals

  • Monthly plans: we do not refund a month that has started. Cancel before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Yearly plans: write to us within 14 days after a yearly renewal and we refund it in full. After that we do not refund the rest of the year, but you keep your plan until it ends.

4. Changing plans

When you upgrade, the price difference for the rest of the period is added to your next invoice. When you downgrade, the change starts at the end of the period you paid for, so there is nothing to refund.

5. When something went wrong on our side

If we charged you by mistake (for example twice), if SafeFix was down for a long time, or if we removed something important from your paid plan, write to us. We refund the amount that was affected.

6. Consumers in the EU and the UK

If you buy as a consumer, the law may give you the right to cancel within 14 days. A paid plan starts right away, so you ask us to begin at once when you subscribe. Our 14-day refund on your first payment (above) still gives you your money back if you change your mind.

7. How refunds are paid

We refund through Stripe, to the card or account you paid with, in US dollars. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days. If your bank converts the amount to another currency, your bank sets the exchange rate and any fee.

8. Contact

Questions about refunds: support@safefix.dev.
