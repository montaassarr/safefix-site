1. This agreement

These terms are a contract between you and SafeFix (“we”), the service at safefix.dev. By creating an account or using SafeFix, you accept them. If you use SafeFix for a company, you accept them for that company and confirm that you may do so.

How we handle personal data is in our privacy policy. Refunds are in our refund policy.

2. What SafeFix does

SafeFix checks apps for security problems (database rules, leaked keys, Docker, GitHub Actions and app settings), explains each one in plain English, and suggests a fix. Scans with the CLI or inside your AI tool run on your own computer. The web dashboard keeps the results you upload, so your team can track them.

3. Your account

  • Use a real email address and keep your sign-in safe.
  • You are responsible for what happens in your organization, including the people you add and the upload tokens you create. Revoke a token as soon as you think it leaked.
  • Only owners and admins of an organization can change its plan and billing.

4. Scan only what you may

Use SafeFix only on apps, databases, code and accounts that you own, or that you have written permission to test. You must not:

  • use SafeFix to attack, break into or overload any system;
  • try to reach other organizations’ data, or get around our limits or security;
  • upload data that you are not allowed to share;
  • resell SafeFix without our written agreement.

5. Plans and payment

  • The Free plan costs nothing. Paid plans have the limits shown on our pricing page.
  • Paid plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, in US dollars, by our payment provider Stripe. They renew automatically until you cancel. Taxes may be added depending on where you are.
  • Upgrades start at once; the price difference for the rest of the period is added to your next invoice. Downgrades start at the end of the period you paid for.
  • If a payment fails, you keep your plan for 7 days while Stripe tries again. After that you are on Free until the payment goes through. Your data is not deleted.
  • We tell you at least 30 days before a price change on your plan. It applies from your next renewal.

6. Cancelling

You can cancel anytime in Settings, Plan and billing, Manage billing. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, then you are on Free. Refunds follow our refund policy.

7. Fixes are suggestions

  • SafeFix never changes your app, code or data. You decide which fix to apply, and you should test it first.
  • No tool finds every problem. A good grade means SafeFix found no serious problem in what it checks. It does not mean your app is fully secure.

8. Your content

Your code, your data and your scan results stay yours. You allow us to store and process them only to run SafeFix for you, as the privacy policy explains. If you send us ideas or feedback, we may use them without owing you anything.

9. Availability and changes

We work to keep SafeFix running, but we cannot promise it is never down, for example during maintenance. We may change or remove features. If a change removes something important from a paid plan, we tell you first, and you may cancel and ask for a refund for the unused time.

10. No warranty

SafeFix is provided “as is” and “as available”, without warranties of any kind, as far as the law allows. If you are a consumer, you keep every right that the law of your country gives you.

11. Limits of our liability

As far as the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses (such as lost profit or lost data), or for security problems in your app that SafeFix did not find. Our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

12. Suspension and ending

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, for example to stop an attack or abuse. When we can, we warn you first. You can stop using SafeFix and ask us to delete your account at any time. After an account ends, we delete its data as the privacy policy explains.

13. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. For important changes we email organization owners at least 30 days before they start. If you keep using SafeFix after that, the new terms apply.

14. Law and disputes

If something goes wrong, write to us first: most problems are solved by email. If we cannot agree, the dispute goes to the courts that the law names. If you are a consumer, you can always use the courts of the country where you live.

15. Contact

Questions about these terms: support@safefix.dev.
